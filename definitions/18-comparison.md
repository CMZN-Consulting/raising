# comparison

Version 0.1.0. Depends on: trace, progress, verdict.

Two valid traces under the same version compare by progress. The comparison is decided when their brackets do not overlap, and undecided otherwise. Undecided is reported as undecided.

Two individuals compare only as two records at two times under a declared grounding, which is a measure or a judge. "More mature under this grounding" is a statement about two records, never about two agents. Changing the grounding may change the order.

Equality holds in one case only: two records that replay to the same state.

The general comparison of individuals, under no grounding or under every judge at once, is open at the judge. Claims about it are made per judge, as a schema, and measured per judge.
