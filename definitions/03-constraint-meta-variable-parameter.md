# constraint, meta-variable, parameter

Depends on: object.

A constraint is one listed property of an object. An object's constraints are its axioms in the sense of the axioms of a group: they define the object, and every point of it shows each one. The word "axiom" is nonetheless not used for a constraint, because in Lean that word names something assumed, and a constraint is never assumed of a candidate. It is shown.

A meta-variable is a part of an object's listing that is left for a point to fill: a name standing where a thing is required, such as a function, a bound, a judge or a length. An object with a meta-variable is a family of objects, one per fill.

A parameter is a meta-variable that may be filled by any thing of a declared kind. The constraints speak of it by name, and whatever is shown about the object is shown for every fill of the parameter at once. A number or a name that is a parameter is admitted in a definition; one that is neither a parameter nor forced by a showing raises the first flag of the bar (25).
