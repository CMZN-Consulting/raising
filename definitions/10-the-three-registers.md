# the three registers

Depends on: the gate.

Every claim in this framework is made in exactly one of three registers.

Proved: a theorem checked by the Lean kernel through the gate (09). Its hypotheses are part of the claim.

Measured: a quantity observed by a stated procedure under stated conditions, reported with its seed and its uncertainty. A measured claim never becomes a proved one by repetition.

Recorded: a fact read from a record that a checker replays. A recorded claim is about that record, at that time.

A sentence that states a claim names its register. A sentence that says "proved" of a measured or recorded claim is wrong, whatever else it gets right. A claim marked "to be proved" names its register and says that the kernel has not yet checked it; until it has, the claim stands as stated and nothing rests on it. What is neither proved, measured nor recorded is not a claim of the framework; if it is said at all, it is said as a belief and marked as one (11).
