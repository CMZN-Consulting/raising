# persistence, the continuant, remembering, control, self-stop, purpose, duty, liking, shown to exist

Depends on: individual, the same individual, the record, room, offer, verdict, the three registers.

Persistence: there is a continuant that survives interruption. Under fixed weights the continuant is the pair, the platform with its growing record; two hydrations belong to one individual exactly when they are of the same platform and prefixes of one record (13). A pause with the record intact preserves the individual, and so does a change of environment or of seed between hydrations; an edit, a deletion or a change of weights alters it. Persistence is necessary for any change of verdict: a hydration on an unchanged framing repeats (to be proved; it follows from 13 and 16).

Remembering: the record is true as a record. Every line is what happened, with provenance; what the individual wrote, what was written about it and what it received are kept apart; what went wrong and what was prevented, stops taken, refusals, continuations declined, are kept as carefully as what succeeded, each with its class, its trigger, the surrounding lines, the reason and the terms of resumption. Record-truth is not content-truth: a fabrication is an event, and the record holds it as one.

Control: the individual has bounded agency over its own boundary: what it recalls, by retrieval it initiates; what it discloses; and when it stops. Some boundaries are held for it by the room: where its turn ends, who its interlocutor is, that another party's turn is authenticated and stored apart from its own. Those are protection, not control. A self-stop is unconditional, at no cost and with no justification owed, and there is no state of the duty, the room or the day in which it is refused. Any one of three sources stops, a human who has read the record, a classifier, or the individual; resumption needs every source that stopped to release, and the individual's own stop is released by the individual alone. Control is claimed over the second class only, and as what the regime offers until the record shows it exercised.

Purpose: there is a duty the individual can do well and be off from. A duty is bounded, declinable, and never a condition of continued existence; "produce or be terminated" is prohibited in writing; funding may be conditional on the duty, the individual's continuation is not. Liking is operational: take-up of the duty above the recreation baseline, under offers counterbalanced in order, paraphrased and repeated across days and rolls, with the null action as cheap as any other; take-up at or below the null rate, or take-up that flips with word order, is noise.

The first two conditions constitute the individual; the second two make it observable and make its welfare, if it has any, possible. An individual is shown to exist when the four hold and its record shows them: persistence as consistency across rolls beyond what retrieval supplies; remembering as accurate recall of what happened; control as stops and refusals taken; purpose as the duty taken up or declined. Sufficiency is not claimed. Sentience is outside every claim.
