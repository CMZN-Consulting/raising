# Theta, Outer-Frame, legal, run

Depends on: object, instance, arrow. Lean: `Raising.Theta`, `Raising.OuterFrame`, `Raising.OuterFrame.run`, `Raising.OuterFrame.run_add`, `Raising.OuterFrame.arrow`, in `Raising/Object.lean` and `Raising/OuterFrame.lean`.

Theta is the outer frame's time. A value of Theta is a natural number under the name Theta, and it counts the steps the outer frame has taken; every natural number is a value. One unit of Theta is one step, so the number five is a value of Theta, five steps, and not a unit; as with a token (02), what makes a number a value of Theta is its use. Nothing of any individual enters it; what an individual does during one step is the components' matter, not the root's.

An Outer-Frame is an environment that runs a simulation, taken as the object it runs sees it: aligned to an object, it is a step on the object's candidates that keeps the extension, sending every candidate in the extension to a candidate in the extension, with the showing. Keeping the extension is called legality, and it is part of being an outer frame: a step that leaves the extension is no outer frame, and where the files below say "legal outer frame" the word only repeats this. The step is the frame's and not the object's: an object is its carrier and its list of properties, and the step is neither. What the frame does to a candidate, it does by its step; what a candidate can ask of the frame is a call, which is a tool's matter (11).

Running a candidate to the value θ of Theta applies the step θ times: at zero the candidate is as it was, and each further step applies the step once more (proved: `Raising.OuterFrame.run_zero`, `Raising.OuterFrame.run_succ`, by reflexivity). Running for a units and then for b units is running for a + b units (proved: `Raising.OuterFrame.run_add`): the values of Theta act on the candidates through the frame, adding as the runs compose, and this is the sense in which Theta is the frame's time.

An instance run by an outer frame stays in the extension at every value of Theta (proved: `Raising.OuterFrame.burden`, the burden on an object; the burden of the root, over declarations, is 08). The run to any value of Theta is itself an arrow from the object to itself.
