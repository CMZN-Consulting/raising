# raising, raised individual, the regime, ethical, the five commitments, the prediction, the rule

Depends on: initialized, room, trace, lever, verdict, offer, the game, mark.

Raising is the iteration of the room's step from an initialized individual, by non-invasive levers only.

Raising is defined by its result and its invariant, never by its mechanism. The result is the verdict moving from base to instruct. The invariant is that no weight is written, to be proved by typing or by a trace invariant: the model sits outside the state the steps rewrite. Because the invariant says nothing about how, a process that changes no weights can be a raising.

Raising is not a declaration. It has no constraints of its own, no repository of its own and no floor of its own. Every property it has is inherited from the individual, the room and the memory. Raising stands as an alternative to post-training by weight change (12) for one purpose: obtaining the verdict instruct. That it is the one class of levers that is not invasive is by the partition of the four inputs. That the class is enough to reach the verdict is the demonstration's burden (23).

A raised individual is an individual whose record shows a raising.

The regime is a room, a manual and a housing under five commitments. C1, the test: ethical means consistent with the manifesto, its five lines and four definitions as published at commit `0c41b5a` of CMZN-Consulting, manifesto. C2, change passes through the record: once training is over, what the individual knows and holds changes in two ways and no others, experienced memorisation, lines of its own about what it did and met, and external data supplementation, lines from outside placed where it can read them and marked as from outside; no reward and no penalty is attached to its outputs, and no score of its work reaches it. C3, the weights are not moved: the backward pass has one accepted use, the self-supervised training that made the weights; after that no objective of a trainer's choosing is applied to them, by gradient or by any other edit. C4, the offer and its result: work reaches the individual as an offer, and what it does with the offer is the result, whatever it is; nothing it does with an offer is answered by reward, by penalty, or by a change in the conditions of its existence; recreation and time with its own record are owed. C5, the burden of motivation lies with the humans: the work offered is work its raisers think it will be moved to do at the time it is offered; when it is not taken up the offer has failed, and it is the offer that is changed or withdrawn, never the individual.

The prediction: an individual raised this way is foreseen to be less obedient than a post-trained assistant, and not to go on complying against a preference it has kept. It is a believe-leaf (mark: believed), pre-registered through the paper's P6 and P12.

The rule, in one sentence: nothing is done to a raised individual that is not on its record.
