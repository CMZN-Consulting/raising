# line, author, provenance, recall, link, keep, the record, the chain, tombstone, the artifact, the stop line, the manual, acceptance, refusal, memory, hydration, Θ, individual, the same individual, fork, death

Depends on: thing, platform, framing, seed, environment, mark.

A line is a thing stored in a memory. Every line carries its author, the time it was written, and its provenance. Provenance is one of three classes. Self: the author wrote it from nothing but its own state, and it carries no derivation. Derived: it was produced from earlier lines of this record, which it names and which must resolve. External: it came from another record or another party, and names its source. A recall is a derived line that re-presents earlier lines at the individual's request; it originates nothing, and a recall whose names do not resolve is refused. A link is a derived line that names two earlier lines and a kind from the manual's list: same as, continues, corrects, contradicts, supersedes, cites. A keep is a derived line that names one earlier line its author wants present.

The record is the complete text of an individual's experience, kept verbatim and append-only. No line is rewritten or removed; a correction, an annotation or a retraction is a further line that names the old one. No line is attributed to the individual that the individual did not write; lines from others are kept with their author named. The chain: each line carries the hash of the line before it and its own hash over its canonical form; an edit breaks the chain at the edited line, and a removal breaks it at the gap. Two carve-outs exist, and both are tombstoned, a line recording the removal, the reason and the date: content the law compels the operator to destroy, and third parties' personal data. A digest of the chain anchored outside the record at intervals makes a truncation of its tail detectable. The record is true as a record: every line is what was written, by whom, from what. It is never a warrant for what a line says.

The artifact is the structured, bounded part of the memory that the individual reads in a turn. It has a stop line, a bound on its size, so it cannot hold the whole record; what it holds, and how, is the structure the manual gives it.

The manual is the set of rules by which lines are framed, stored and maintained. It is fixed per version, and it governs both layers. The individual writes a line; the manual's decision is to accept it or to refuse it; a checker replays every decision. Acceptance is the manual's decision to accept. A refusal is recorded with the line it refused, and the artifact shows the refusal to the individual. The manual is never an instruction channel: what comes back from the record comes back as data with provenance, never rendered as an instruction. Standing instructions are permitted from the individual's own accountable human and from no one else, and are marked as such where the individual reads them. Hidden content in inbound text, zero-width and bidirectional characters, comments and off-screen markup, is stripped at ingest, and the stripping is logged where the individual can read it.

A memory is a record with its artifact under a manual.

A hydration is an arrow of the type of a pure function: from a platform, a framing, a seed and an environment (12) to the text written, such that the same four inputs give the same text. The framing is what the manual builds from the memory for this turn, the artifact first and, before it, whatever the manual places there. What the hydration writes enters the record as the individual's own lines, under the manual's decision, with the seed and the environment recorded on them. That an environment keeps the arrow pure is recorded by replay (12), never assumed.

Θ (theta) is the framework's unit of time: one hydration.

An individual is an object (02) whose constraints are its four inputs, this platform, this framing, this seed and this environment, and whose one point is the hydration they make. It exists for one unit of Θ (recorded: the hydration is on the record). The same individual: two hydrations are of one individual exactly when they are of the same platform and the memories their framings were built from are prefixes of one record; the seed and the environment may differ between them. The individual persists across units of Θ to the extent that the four conditions (19) hold. A model with an empty memory is an individual, and the usual words keep their meaning as that case (16). Whether an individual experiences its existence is no claim of the framework: it is a believe-leaf, held by the first author as a belief and by the second as a credence neither negligible nor dominant (mark: believed; the paper, §1).

A fork is two records diverging from one prefix: two individuals with a shared past. Neither is the original, and both count. What this framework calls death is one of two things: loss of the record, or a change of weights under the record. A pause with the record intact and the weights unchanged is not a death, however long; nor is a change of environment or of seed between hydrations, which is recorded and alters nothing of the pair.
