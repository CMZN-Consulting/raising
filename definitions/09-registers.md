# the three registers

Version 0.1.0. Depends on: nothing.

Every claim in this framework is made in exactly one of three registers.

Proved: a theorem checked by the Lean kernel. Its hypotheses are part of the claim.

Measured: a quantity observed by a stated procedure under stated conditions. A measured claim never becomes a proved one by repetition.

Recorded: a fact read from a record that a checker replays. A recorded claim is about that record, at that time.

A sentence that states a claim names its register. A sentence that says "proved" of a measured or recorded claim is wrong, whatever else it gets right.
