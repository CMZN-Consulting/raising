# Definitions

**Not done yet, and coming soon.**

A first draft of twenty definitions stood here from 2026-10-03 to 2026-10-04. It was withdrawn from the tree when the white paper was published. It is still in this repository's history, at commit `9e613ae` and before, and nothing about its having been here is hidden.

The reason is plain. The framework is being redefined, and the design has moved since the draft was written, so several of its files no longer said what the design says. The subject deserves better than a half-finished text standing as the public one, and the time is better spent on the definitions than on patching the draft.

When the definitions come back they will be read in order, each will name what it depends on, and each will state the register of every claim it makes: proved, measured, or recorded.
