# thing

Version 0.1.0. Depends on: nothing.

A thing is a body of text. Everything this framework speaks of is a thing.

A thing is an atom or a composite. An atom is a piece of text or a name. A composite is a tag together with a list of things.

What varies between things is who reads them: a proof checker, an evaluator, a language model, or a person. Anything outside the framework, such as a model's weights, enters as a name, never as a body.

The set of tags grows by version. The notion of thing does not change.
