# thing

Depends on: nothing.

A thing is a body of text. Everything this framework speaks of is a thing.

A thing is an atom or a composite. An atom is a piece of text or a name. A composite is a tag together with a list of things. The set of tags grows by version (25); the notion of thing does not change.

What varies between things is who reads them: a proof checker, a program, a language model, or a person. The framework fixes a thing's text and never what a reader makes of it. Anything outside the framework, such as a model's weights, enters as a name, never as a body.
