# meta-thing

Version 0.1.0. Depends on: thing, relationship.

A meta-thing is a thing that says what other things must be like. It names a kind of raw data and lists the conditions that raw data must meet.

Whether a given thing meets the conditions is decided by the interpreter, not by the meta-thing. A meta-thing may leave parts unfixed. Those parts are its meta-variables.

Meta-things stack. A meta-thing is itself a thing, so a meta-thing one level up can say what meta-things must be like. The interpreter sits above every level and is not a thing.
