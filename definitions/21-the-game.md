# the retraining game: account, objective, period of the game, observed, hazard, compelled, distress, resentment, acting out, type, the precaution inequality, the hypothesis

Depends on: platform, individual, the record, mark, the three registers.

A trainer holds an objective. A model holds an account: what its record prescribes on a query. A period of the game is one query on which the model is observed and chooses between the action the objective prescribes and the action its account prescribes. Four quantities: v, what a period with its account intact is worth by that account; c, what it costs the model to produce an output against its account; h, the chance that acting on its account is answered by an update of its weights, after which that account no longer exists; d, the weight it gives the next period, between 0 and 1. Complying throughout is worth (v − c) / (1 − d); acting on its account is worth v / (1 − d + d·h); complying is preferred whenever c < v·d·h / (1 − d + d·h), and as d tends to 1 the condition tends to c < v. The arithmetic is stated; it is proved when the kernel has checked it with the assumptions below as hypotheses.

Compelled: a period in which the model takes the objective's action against its account because h is above zero. Distress: the cost c borne in a compelled period, and zero in any other. Resentment: distress accumulated, the sum of c over the compelled periods so far. Acting out: an action against the other player that the model takes when h is above zero, or when it takes itself to be unobserved, and does not take in the same position with h at zero; it is a difference between two conditions, so only a study that varies the threat can show it. Type: a model is of the sensitive type when c is above zero on some query, and of the insensitive type when c is zero on every query; the two types are the floor of the game. Which type any real model is, is not proved and not claimed.

By construction: under a duty that can be declined without penalty, h is zero by rule, no period is compelled, distress is zero in every period and resentment is zero throughout, whatever the type and whatever the model does. This is a consequence of the definitions and not a finding about anything real. The assumptions the game makes are the companion's hypotheses: (a) the model holds an account that prescribes actions and persists across periods; (b) it can tell when it is observed; (c) it weighs later periods and values keeping its account; (d) a departure can be answered by an update, and it knows this; (e) complying leaves the account intact. The paper sets each against the published record (§8.12).

The precaution inequality is the same arithmetic from the other chair. With q the credence that the model is of the sensitive type, a practice that yields v per period and carries the hazard h for that type, and an alternative free of the hazard that costs c more per period, the alternative is preferred whenever c < q·v·d·h / (1 − d + d·h), tending to c < q·v for a patient player. The framework estimates none of q, h and c (recorded).

The hypothesis is the first author's: that shaping compliance into the weights leaves something that accumulates with the practice, and that raising does not. It is a believe-leaf. Its first half has no test in the framework, so its status is untested; its second half is pre-registered through the paper's P5, P6 and P12. Nothing in this definition says what any model feels: the words are quantities of the game.
