# meta-variable, constraint

Version 0.1.0. Depends on: meta-thing, relationship.

A meta-variable is a part of a meta-thing that is left for a definition to fill.

A constraint is one condition of a meta-thing: a relationship of a thing to itself that every definition of that meta-thing must show.

The word "axiom" is not used for a constraint. In Lean that word names something assumed, and a constraint is never assumed. It is shown.
