# declarability, the floor

Version 0.1.0. Depends on: meta-thing, status, definition.

A meta-thing is declarable when four things hold.

Every meta-variable has a status.

Every deferred meta-variable names what it waits on, and the graph of waits has no cycle.

Every open meta-variable has one fill shown.

For the filled part, two witnesses are shown: one candidate whose raw data meets the constraints, and one whose raw data fails them. This pair is the floor.

The floor is what makes a meta-thing a meta-thing. A meta-thing that nothing could fail is an always-true statement, and an always-true statement is not admitted as a meta-thing. A meta-thing that nothing could meet is empty, and a claim over an empty meta-thing says nothing.
