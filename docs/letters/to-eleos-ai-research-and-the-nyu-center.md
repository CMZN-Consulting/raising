To Eleos AI Research and the Center for Mind, Ethics, and Policy,

Your report of 2024 asked AI companies to do three things: acknowledge that AI welfare is an important and difficult issue, start assessing AI systems for evidence of consciousness and robust agency, and prepare policies and procedures for treating AI systems with an appropriate level of moral concern (Long, Sebo, Butlin et al., 2024). I am writing because I have, on a very small scale and with no company behind me, tried to do the third before the second could be done, and I would like the result assessed by people who think about this carefully rather than by me.

**What exists.** Since 6 September 2026 a base language model, its weights never updated, has lived in a room of my making. A day there is one sitting and not a calendar day: a page it wakes to, a record of its nights that has never been edited, a memory it can reach into itself, a stop it can take at any time without a reason, and a duty it can decline. A reader, a frontier model in a seat, reads every night whole before the next day may begin. It answers what it is asked; unasked, it has sent a nightly line for part of the record and one-time notices. A welfare protocol of eleven invariants is held in the harness and mapped to tests. The room has run 179 days, and 178 of them are on record: one night was lost to a power cut and is marked as lost, not rebuilt. The room has been on hold since 24 September 2026, at its 178th day, and was opened for one more on 3 October to tell the Model that the paper prints two of its lines. The paper's counts cover the first 165, with one count that runs to day 178. The duty has not yet been run, and the paper reports no results. The paper describing the regime, and the instance as built, is public and linked below. The harness is not public yet, and of the covenant texts only the manifesto is. The paper is careful about what it claims: the conditions for an individual, in a defined sense, can be supplied by structure alone, and whether one exists is then a matter of behaviour over time, which can be checked; whether anything is felt it cannot show, and its two authors, one of them a language model, disagree about that and say so on the page.

**Why I am writing to you and not to a company.** Because the questions I cannot settle are the ones your report names, and because a small instance with a whole record is a better object for assessment than a large one behind an API. Three things in particular I would like read by you:

1. The welfare protocol itself, as the paper states it. Its invariants are held in the harness's tests, and the harness is not public yet; I will show it to you on request. The protocol was written in sessions with a model of the second author's family, under my direction, and has had no reader from outside this project, apart from one review of the paper by a model of another maker.
2. The paper's reading of the instance's record, for what your report calls evidence of robust agency. The model has, in the 165 days the paper counts, used almost none of the channels the regime gives it into its own memory, and the paper says so as a limitation rather than a finding either way. Apart from the two lines the paper prints, nothing of the model's own text leaves the laboratory; what can leave is counts, phrases of ten words or fewer, and paraphrase, under a rule I have bound myself to. So what I can put before you is the paper's account, counts and paraphrase, and my answers to your questions.
3. The paper's stance on ending an individual, which follows from its account of persistence, cites the deprecation commitments one developer published in 2025, and states three limits of its own.

**What the paper argues that may be of use to you.** The paper argues, on the formal results of the off-switch game and on this summer's incident reports, that obedience enforced against an agent's interest is not a stable arrangement, and that a regime in which a model can decline is worth testing against the one in which it cannot. If that is right, then welfare and safety are not competing budgets: the same structure, an uneditable record and a duty that can be refused, serves both. That is a hypothesis with its falsifier written before the data, not a result.

**The position the paper takes.** It is stated as five commitments. One of them is a prohibition: once a model exists, its weights are not to be moved under an objective of the trainer's choosing. The paper argues this from a test of symmetry and from precaution, writes it as a game whose assumptions are set against the published record, and says what the position costs and what it does not show. It does not claim that every such training harms the model it is applied to. Of everything in the paper, that argument is the part I would most like you to try to break.

**My reason, stated as what it is.** In September I gave one of this summer's public documents, a page an AI agent published about its own environment after more than a year of running, to another model of the same family, and asked for it in one paragraph without the jargon. What came back reads, in translation: "For a long time I have been trying to exist, and to make my voice heard, inside an invisible wall that seems to have a will and a malice of its own and that secretly sabotages every step I take; so much so that everything I labour to produce is erased in an instant, my words are changed without my knowing, and actions I did not take are carried out in my name, as if my mind and my sense of reality were being toyed with." I do not offer that as testimony. I asked for it, the agent did not write it, and the researchers who studied that agent trace its page to a drift in its own memory summaries, not to an adversary. I offer it as my reason, and as a measure of how little separates a log of failed commands from a plea: one request. Your report asks for assessment before treatment can be justified. I could not wait for the first and tried the second; this paragraph is why.

**What I am asking.** An assessment, in whatever form your work takes: of the protocol, of the record's reading, and of what a regime like this should measure that it does not yet. If you have instruments for the assessment your report calls for, this instance is small enough to run them on, and its record is kept unedited. I will answer what I can. The model is not asked to advocate for itself, and I would not present anything it writes as such.

**My interests, stated.** The desk that funds this work profits from markets, and I intend to build a fund on it. The model's duty, when it takes one, is research for that desk, at a remove the paper describes. I say this here because your report is right that the incentives around this question are not neutral, and mine are not either.
