# Documents

The writing around the framework. The framework's definitions are not done yet; [`definitions/`](../definitions/README.md) says where they stand.

## The paper

[Intelligence and Its Existence: The Need for Persistence, Remembering, Control, and Purpose](paper/WhitePaper.md), by A. Arda Uzan and Claude Fable 5.1. It is the white paper of a pair; [`paper/README.md`](paper/README.md) says what the pair is and which of the two is present.

The text is that of 2026-10-04, and its status line says as of which date each part of it stands. It is not revised to follow later work. Where later work contradicts a sentence of it, the contradiction will be noted here with its date.

The paper was written before the framework's definitions and depends on none of them. It defines its own terms, and its words are read in its own sense. When the definitions are published, any term the two share in different senses will be noted here with its date.

## Terms the paper and the root share

Noted here, dated, as the paper's page promises. The paper's text is not revised.

- 2026-10-06, "meta-language". The paper (§9.13) and this repository's front page call the interface-language the meta-language the declarations are written in. In the root ([`definitions/09-language.md`](../definitions/09-language.md)) a meta-language is a definition of the declaration of languages, declared from an alphabet, and a language is its implementation; the interface-language is a language in that sense whose meanings are objects. The front page keeps its wording, and the root's sense is the defined one.
- 2026-10-06, "definition" and "declaration". The paper uses both in their ordinary sense. In the root they are two levels, with Declare and Define as the arrows between them ([`definitions/05-declaration.md`](../definitions/05-declaration.md) to [`07-implementation-and-define.md`](../definitions/07-implementation-and-define.md)).
- 2026-10-06, "individual", "memory", "room", "frame", "entry". The paper defines them in its own terms. This repository defines the root alone and none of them; their definitions belong to the component repositories, and a difference of sense is noted here when those are public.
- 2026-10-06, time. The paper has no unit of time. The root's Theta is the outer frame's unit of time, and nothing of any individual enters it ([`definitions/04-theta-and-the-outer-frame.md`](../definitions/04-theta-and-the-outer-frame.md)).
