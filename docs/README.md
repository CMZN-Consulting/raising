## The paper

The text is that of 2026-10-04, and its status line says as of which date each part of it stands. It is not revised to follow later work. Where later work contradicts a sentence of it, the contradiction will be noted here with its date.

The paper was written before the framework's definitions and depends on none of them. It defines its own terms, and its words are read in its own sense. When the definitions are published, any term the two share in different senses will be noted here with its date.
